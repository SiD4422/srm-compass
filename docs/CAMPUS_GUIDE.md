# SRMIST Campuses: Transit, Location & Decision Guide (2026–2027)

Prospective engineering students choosing between SRMIST campuses face significant differences in campus environment, commuter access, annual tuition, and programme breadth. All six campuses participate in the centralized admissions process.

## Campus Details & Commuter Context

### 1. Kattankulathur (Main Campus)
- **Environment**: Flagship residential campus housing over 40,000 students, high-performance computing clusters, 15+ student hostels, central hospital, and startup incubators.
- **Commuter Access**:
  - **Suburban Rail**: Potheri Railway Station is located directly opposite the main university gate, providing direct EMU local trains to Chennai Beach, Chennai Egmore, and Tambaram.
  - **Airport**: Chennai International Airport (MAA) is approximately 22 km north via the Grand Southern Trunk (GST) Road (NH 45).
  - **Bus Network**: Direct state transport and university fleet routes across Chennai and Chengalpattu.
- **Decision Factor**: Recommended for students seeking the complete residential university experience, specialized engineering branches (Aerospace, Biomedical, Nanotechnology, Genetic Engineering), and active hackathon/racing teams (e.g. Camber Racing).

### 2. Ramapuram (Urban Campus)
- **Environment**: High-density urban campus located near the DLF IT corridor in western Chennai, well-suited for day scholars and students seeking proximity to IT industry parks.
- **Commuter Access**:
  - **Metro**: Alandur Metro Interchange (Green/Blue Line) is ~4 km away; Guindy Metro is ~5 km away.
  - **Airport**: Chennai International Airport (MAA) is ~10 km away (20–25 minutes driving time).
  - **Road**: Mount-Poonamallee High Road arterial link.
- **Decision Factor**: Strong option for students focusing on core Computer Science, IT, and Electronics who prefer an urban Chennai location with tuition generally lower than the KTR flagship campus.

### 3. Vadapalani (City Center Campus)
- **Environment**: Vertical metropolis campus located on Inner Ring Road (100 Feet Road), surrounded by commercial and media hubs.
- **Commuter Access**:
  - **Metro**: Vadapalani Metro Station (Green Line) is situated less than 300 meters from the entrance.
  - **Bus**: Adjacent to the Vadapalani Central Bus Terminus connecting all Chennai zones.
  - **Airport**: Direct Metro connection to Chennai International Airport (~11 km).
- **Decision Factor**: Best choice for Chennai city residents who wish to avoid daily highway commutes while pursuing B.Tech in CSE, AI & Data Science, or Computer & Communication Engineering.

### 4. Tiruchirappalli (Trichy Campus)
- **Environment**: Expansive modern campus on the Chennai-Trichy National Highway (NH 45), featuring state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure serving central and southern Tamil Nadu.
- **Commuter Access**:
  - **Airport**: Tiruchirappalli International Airport (TRZ) is 20 km away, providing direct connections to international hubs (Singapore, Dubai, Colombo).
  - **Rail**: Tiruchirappalli Junction (TPJ), a major Southern Railway junction, is ~16 km away.
- **Decision Factor**: Offers significantly lower annual tuition fees (₹1.25L to ₹2.25L) for students seeking accredited SRM degrees with modern hostel amenities closer to central Tamil Nadu.

### 5. Delhi-NCR (Modinagar Campus)
- **Environment**: Established 25-acre green residential campus situated on the Delhi-Meerut highway, serving northern India students since 1997.
- **Commuter Access**:
  - **Rapid Transit**: Proximity to the Delhi-Meerut Regional Rapid Transit System (Namo Bharat RRTS) corridor.
  - **Expressway**: Direct access via the Delhi-Meerut Expressway.
  - **Rail**: Ghaziabad Junction (28 km) and New Delhi Railway Station (50 km).
- **Decision Factor**: Provides full access to SRMJEEE counselling and placement networks without requiring relocation to southern India.

### 6. Baburayanpettai Campus
- **Environment**: Emerging campus development situated in the rural Chengalpattu district belt with a focus on core foundational engineering disciplines.
- **Commuter Access**:
  - **Rail**: Serviced via Chengalpattu Railway Junction (~18 km) and Madurantakam.
  - **Road**: Accessible through regional connectors off the GST corridor.
- **Decision Factor**: Represents the most accessible tuition structure in the SRM system (₹1.00L to ₹1.50L per year) for core engineering degrees.
